RANKHERON WEBSITE: HOW TO GO LIVE
=================================

1. Fill in the blanks (search each file for "[")
   - index.html, privacy.html, terms.html: company name, company number,
     registered office address, and "England and Wales" if that's wrong.
   - privacy.html: the services you use (email, form, web host, accounts)
     and how long you keep data.
   - terms.html: VAT wording, payment terms, liability cap, and whether
     articles published during a free trial are the client's to keep.
   Have the privacy policy and terms checked before relying on them.

2. Choose where trial sign-ups go (main.js, near the top)
   - Leave FORM_ENDPOINT empty: visitors' email app opens with their
     details filled in, addressed to hello@rankheron.com.
   - Or create a free form at formspree.io, paste its address
     (https://formspree.io/f/...) into FORM_ENDPOINT, and sign-ups
     arrive in your inbox with no extra step for the visitor.
   Make sure hello@rankheron.com actually receives email.

3. Upload everything in this folder to the root of rankheron.com,
   keeping the "fonts" folder as it is. No build step is needed.

4. After it's live
   - Visit https://rankheron.com/robots.txt and check it shows "Allow: /".
   - Add the site in Google Search Console and submit
     https://rankheron.com/sitemap.xml.
   - Paste https://rankheron.com into Facebook's Sharing Debugger
     (developers.facebook.com/tools/debug) and click "Scrape Again" so
     Facebook picks up the preview image.

5. Reviews badge
   When you have real reviews, paste your Trustpilot or Google reviews
   widget code into the empty <div id="reviews" hidden> under the hero
   form in index.html, delete the word "hidden", and add the widget's
   provider to the privacy policy.
