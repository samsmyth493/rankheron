The problem

SEO works. Doing it yourself doesn't.

Ranking takes research, writing, optimisation and patience, every single week. Most businesses try one of three routes, then stall.

Route 01

Hire an agency

Strategy and reports, but usually locked into a long contract with a retainer you can't easily tie to results.

Route 02

Hire a freelance writer

Good words, but someone still has to pick the keywords, optimise every page and get it published.

Route 03

Ask ChatGPT

Fast, but generic. Without research and editing, raw AI drafts rarely rank, and they never publish themselves.

RankHeron gives you the agency's strategy, the writer's craft and AI's speed for one flat monthly fee.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Send us your website, a competitor and your email. No card needed. Over the next 14 days you get your competitor audit, your keyword plan and your first articles in review, so you can judge the work before you pay anything.

What happens when the 14 days are up?

We'll check in before your trial ends. If you want to carry on, choose a plan and we'll send your first invoice. If not, just let us know. We never took a card, so there's nothing to cancel.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. Google itself says no one can guarantee a #1 ranking. What we guarantee is the work: how many articles are published, when they go live, and a transparent report of exactly what changed.

Do you use bot traffic, click schemes or link exchanges?

Never. Some services pad reports with automated visits or paid search clicks, and some swap links automatically between their customers' sites. Google's spam policies treat machine-generated traffic and excessive link exchanges as manipulation, and the numbers they produce mean nothing for your business. We never buy traffic, clicks or links: the growth in your report comes from real people finding you in search.

How long until I see results?

Most sites see new keywords appear in Google Search Console within two to three months, with traffic building from month four onwards as your content library grows. Competitive industries can take longer.

What happens after I sign up?

Your competitor audit arrives within 48 hours. Within two working days we'll also send a short onboarding form about your business, customers and brand voice, and ask for access to your website. In week one you get your keyword plan. In week two your first articles go into review.

How much of my time does this take?

About an hour to get started: the onboarding form and a 30-minute kickoff call. After that, as little as you like. Review each article before it goes live, or let us publish automatically.

Is the content written by AI?

We use AI for research and first drafts, then a human editor checks the facts, adds real expertise and matches your brand voice. Google's guidance rewards helpful, people-first content however it's produced, and that's the standard we write to.

Why fewer articles than AI tools?

Because every RankHeron article is researched, edited and fact-checked by a person before it goes live. Many AI autopilot tools publish 30 articles a month with no editor in the loop. Google's spam policies target content mass-produced mainly to rank rather than to help people, and thin pages rarely win customers anyway. We'd rather publish fewer articles that a real buyer finds useful, and that AI assistants trust enough to cite.

What is AI search visibility?

More people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity for recommendations instead of searching. These assistants lean on clear, well-structured, authoritative pages. We track whether they mention your brand and shape your content so it's easy for them to cite.

Which website platforms do you work with?

WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Wix and Squarespace, with direct publishing on most. For anything else, we deliver formatted articles ready to paste in.

Can I approve articles before they go live?

Yes. Review every article first, or switch on auto-publish once you're happy with the quality. Either way, you can request edits at any time.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month-to-month and you can cancel before your next billing date. Everything we've published stays yours.

Do you work with businesses in small towns?

Yes. Smaller towns usually mean less competition, so local businesses there can often rank faster. We focus on your Google Business Profile, local keywords and pages for each area you serve.

Can I resell RankHeron to my own clients?

Yes. The Scale plan includes white-label reports, so agencies and freelancers can offer SEO under their own brand while we handle delivery.
